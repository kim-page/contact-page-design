About office rental in Phnom Penh

Market notes drawn from the 12 verified listings above — observed facts, not estimates.

How much does office space cost in Phnom Penh?

Across the 12 verified options above: coworking from about $88/month (Mekong Space), or $80–150/month with a $10 day pass (Emerald HUB); serviced offices from $349/month (Regus); a 45 sqm serviced office around $1,260/month with furniture, bi-weekly cleaning, receptionist, IT support and meeting-room hours included (The Helix Office); Grade A tower space from about $29/sqm (Canadia Tower). Treat these as observed points across the listed options, not market averages.

What is the difference between Grade A towers, serviced offices and coworking?

Grade A towers — Vattanac Capital (187.3m, the country’s first skyscraper), Canadia Tower and Phnom Penh Tower — are international-standard buildings on the Monivong Boulevard corridor, home to multinationals and professional firms: landmark addresses at premium rents. Serviced offices (Regus, The Helix, Phnom Penh Center) bundle furniture, reception, cleaning and IT into one monthly price with flexible terms — turnkey space, operational on day one. Coworking and creative spaces (Mekong Space, Emerald HUB, Raintree Cambodia, Botanico) sell desks and community by the day or month — the affordable, flexible end.

Where are the main office areas in Phnom Penh?

Preah Monivong Boulevard is the CBD corridor — Vattanac Capital, Canadia Tower, Phnom Penh Tower, The Helix and Regus all sit on or just off it. BKK1 is the lifestyle district (Workspaces at Botanico). Chroy Changvar, across the Tonle Sap — reached by ferry from the riverfront or via the Japanese Friendship Bridge — is the greener, better-value side of the river (Yellow Tower, Mekong Space).

What should I compare before signing?

What “all-in” actually includes varies widely. The Helix lists its bundle explicitly — furniture, bi-weekly cleaning, receptionist, IT support, meeting-room hours. Regus sells flexibility across three city locations including Exchange Square. Compare the bundle line by line, not just the headline rent.
