About real estate agents in Phnom Penh

Market notes drawn from the 12 verified listings above — observed facts, not estimates.

Should I use an agent, or browse listings myself?

Do both — but know the plumbing. Realestate.com.kh (47,000+ listings for sale and rent) is the marketplace most agencies list on, and MLS Cambodia (founded 2012) is the shared inventory backbone agents pool from. The stock overlaps heavily; the differentiator is the agent — area knowledge, negotiation, and paperwork.

Are there international real estate agencies in Phnom Penh?

Yes. Knight Frank runs a permanent local office on the 16th floor of Canadia Tower — the only global consultancy with one in Cambodia, plugged into 370+ offices across 48 countries. ERA and Century 21 operate as international franchises with local teams; Century 21 Imperial Realty is dual-branded with Coldwell Banker.

What do local specialists offer?

Depth in local segments: borey and land corridors (CAM Realty’s Chip Mong, Peng Huoth and Borey Rith focus), Japanese-speaking lines for Japanese buyers and tenants, project sales arms like Casa by Meridian on Diamond Island (Koh Pich), and legacy firms — Bonna Realty (est. 1999) and Khmer Real Estate (since 2007) — with decades of continuous operation.

What does a Phnom Penh agency typically handle?

Sales and rentals at every agency; full-service firms add ownership transfers, valuations and ongoing property management (Khmer Real Estate, Cambodia Trust, Prime Real Estate — the same firm finds, values and manages the asset). Expect to talk on the apps buyers actually use: several agencies operate on Telegram, WhatsApp and WeChat.
